03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity first. Progress every day.

I like understanding how things work — then making something real with what I learn.

I'm Naimul, based in Bangladesh. My path into software has been hands-on: learning web technologies, building small products, and improving through practice.

I'm especially interested in the intersection of software, AI, and robotics. I want to understand the hard problems in this industry and eventually build software that helps intelligent machines work better in the real world.

This site is a living record of that process. Some projects are prototypes and learning exercises; I'm transparent about what they do and how they were built.
